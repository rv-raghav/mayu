---

# Navbar

Transparent blurred navbar.

Left:

- logo

Center:

- Features
- Demo
- Pricing
- Docs

Right:

- Sign In
- Start Free button

---

# Hero Section

Two-column layout.

LEFT:

- headline
- subheadline
- CTA buttons
- social proof

RIGHT:

- animated analytics dashboard preview

Headline:
“Pulse the room. Instantly.”

Subheadline:
“Create elegant live polls, gather responses in real time, and understand your audience with analytics designed for clarity.”

---

# Hero Background

Use:

- subtle paper texture
- soft gradients
- low-opacity particles
- Japanese ink wash visuals

Avoid:

- neon gradients
- over-glow
- cyberpunk visuals

---

# Features Section

3-column card layout.

Features:

- Create in Seconds
- Collect Live Responses
- Understand Instantly

Cards should:

- animate softly on hover
- use subtle icons
- have large spacing

---

# Live Demo Section

Interactive fake poll UI.

Allow:

- clicking poll options
- animated analytics updates
- live counters

This section should feel alive.

---

# Analytics Showcase

Large dashboard preview.

Must include:

- response charts
- realtime counters
- bar charts
- timeline graph
- active poll indicator

The analytics preview is the visual centerpiece of the landing page.

---

# Testimonials

Use:

- editorial cards
- soft backgrounds
- staggered layout

Avoid fake over-the-top testimonials.

---

# Pricing

Two tiers:

- Free
- Pro

Highlight:

- Pro plan subtly

Avoid aggressive pricing design.

---

# CTA Banner

Large centered call-to-action.

Should feel:

- calm
- premium
- confident

---

# Footer

Columns:

- Product
- Resources
- Company
- Legal

Bottom line:
“Crafted for realtime clarity.”
